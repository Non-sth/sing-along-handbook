WHY THIS EXISTS

Off-the-shelf karaoke can't sing "your" songs

Typical karaoke apps

You can only sing what's in their catalog;
audio quality, lyrics and timing are decided by the platform;
rare tracks, live versions and foreign songs are often missing.

This project's way

Take any song you have → AI splits it into instrumental + vocals on your PC → pair it with lyrics to make a "practice page" → load it on your phone and practice anywhere.

Practice pages support: word-by-word highlighting (the syllable being sung lights up), one-tap original / instrumental / vocals switching, tempo control, single-line looping — designed for truly mastering a song.

TWO PARTS

One PC + one phone, each doing its job

🖥️ PC: the separation panel

A page that opens in your browser (127.0.0.1:8848).
Pick a song file, click once, and AI separates instrumental and vocals locally — it can also transcribe lyrics, denoise, and generate practice pages.

Runs fully locally. Nothing gets uploaded to any website.

📱 Phone: the player app

An 8 MB Android app.
Zip the finished "song folder" from your PC, send it to your phone, import — and it becomes a sing-along practice book.

Fully offline, zero storage permission, Android 7.0+.

ABOUT MODELS

Models are the AI's "craft" — download on demand

No expertise needed — follow the colors

Each model in "⑥ Model Library" shows its size and VRAM need, color-coded against your GPU:

🟢 Comfortable go ahead
🟡 Tight runs, but close other apps
🔴 Over budget won't run — alternatives suggested

The default is already strong

The installer ships with the main model, so the full pipeline works without downloading anything else. The library is for those who want to chase even better quality.

Models you download elsewhere are auto-detected once dropped into the model folder.

THE PHONE APP

Install once, then just use it

Install (once only)

Download SingAlong-Player-Android-v1.5.apk from Releases, send it to your phone and tap to install. Allow "unknown sources" when asked (normal for self-signed apps).

Your practice toolkit

One-tap original / instrumental / vocals switching · word-by-word lyric highlighting · slow-down for tricky lines · single-line loop · timeline nudge · lyric search · printable score

Getting songs over: tick "pack mobile zip" when rendering on PC → send via any file-transfer tool / cable / cloud drive → tap "＋ Import song zip" in the app. One zip can hold several songs.

QUICK START

Your first song in three minutes

  1. Install (first time only, ~25–50 min): download SingAlong-Setup-v1.5.exe from Releases and double-click — it sets up the Python environment, AI components and the default model automatically.
  2. Launch the panel: double-click the desktop "Sing-Along" shortcut; the browser opens the panel. A green status bar on top means ready. The 🌐 button (top right) switches 中文/EN.
  3. Run the workflow: in "① Workflow" pick a song, confirm the lyrics source, hit run; then send the zip to your phone, import into the app, and practice.

Stuck? The panel prints the reason in plain language. If it's still stuck, send the on-page log to a tech-savvy friend or an AI assistant.

READ BEFORE USE

Two things, said upfront

⚖️ Copyright
Processing audio you already own for personal practice is fair use; but do not upload or share the separated stems, practice pages or lyric files with others — that's copying and distribution. Generated pages carry a disclaimer in the footer; please keep it.
🎯 Honest limits
AI separation is never 100% clean (a faint vocal ghost may remain in the instrumental) — plenty for practice, but not for publishing covers. It also can't crack encrypted formats like KuGou's .kgm — export normal audio from the client first.
